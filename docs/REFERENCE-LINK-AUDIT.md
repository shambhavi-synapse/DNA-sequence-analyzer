# Molecular Notes reference-link audit

8 November 2026. All 36 unique external destinations in Molecular Notes were inventoried, including the supporting resources and HBB reference. Repeated citations share a single audit result. Direct web retrieval was followed by official publisher, NLM or institutional search when a page returned incomplete content. Source relevance was reviewed against the actual explanation, including its stated research-specific qualifications.

This is not a claim that every link opened successfully. NCBI verification screens, DOI gateways, partial dynamic course pages and a timeout prevent that claim. These environment restrictions are distinguished from confirmed broken links. No evidence established that the retained DOI publications were nonexistent or unrelated. No country-based labels were added.

## Scientific relevance decisions

- RECQL5 is retained only for the explanation's explicit final sentence about stalled-fork remodelling, alongside the foundational replication chapter.
- The mycobacterial topology study supports the specific transcription/topoisomerase extension. It is not presented as the discovery of transcription.
- Rice H4, foxtail-millet transposable-element markers, IndiGenomes, structural variation and hypoxia/splicing papers are linked to the corresponding qualified examples. Their results are not generalized to all organisms.
- The mitochondrial break-repair study is presented as a particular experimental extension, not evidence that every DNA repair pathway works that way.
- NCBI's genetic-code reference directly supports the illustrated UGA assignments. Course resources are further study, not original primary discoveries.

## Remaining access limits

The retained DOI gateways, six Bookshelf chapter URLs, two PubMed routes, the INDA archive and the Nature review require confirmation in an ordinary user browser if they continue to appear blank. Indexed metadata/full text establishes their scientific identities but cannot prove their direct availability to every reader. No paywall, CAPTCHA or access safeguard was bypassed. These limits do not affect SeqScope's local calculations.
